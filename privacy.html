Privacy Policy

Your privacy and security matter.

GameChanger is committed to protecting your personal data and ensuring the secure delivery of our adaptive gaming controller software.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information you voluntarily provide when signing up for early access or contacting us through our web form: name, email address, inquiry type, and message content.

How We Use Data

We use contact form submissions solely to reply to inquiries, manage early access requests, and improve our product. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties except as needed to process form submissions via our trusted service provider.

Security Practices

We host the website over HTTPS, use secure form submission endpoints, and limit data retention to what's necessary for product communication. We also provide download checksum verification so users can validate that installer files have not been tampered with.

Downloads & File Safety

Our Windows and Mac installers are digitally delivered through secure HTTPS links and verified with published SHA-256 checksums on the download page. We do not bundle adware, spyware, or unrelated software.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us through the website form or by email. We will respond promptly to requests from users and partners.

Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or security concerns, please use the contact form on the homepage or email us at contact@usegamechanger.com.
