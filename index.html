Why We Built This

Gaming is connection.
Everyone deserves access.

Ms. Qu used to play video games weekly with friends and family. But as her Parkinson's worsened, so did her ability to use a controller or keyboard—and with that, she lost a form of connection.

She's far from alone. Traditional controllers are designed for performance, not accessibility: clustered buttons, sensitive joysticks, and no flexibility. GameChanger was built to change that.

The Controller

One complete system.
Works in minutes.

3D-printed, fully modular, and powered by a Pro Micro microcontroller — plug in via USB HID and you're ready to play with an adaptive gaming controller designed for accessible gameplay.

Features

Designed for comfort.
Built for independence.

25 Distinct Button Options

Large, easy-to-press controls with low actuation force — choose what works best for you.

100% Modular Design

Every button and joystick screws out and swaps in. 3 different joystick attachments included.

Intuitive Companion App

Remap any button, adjust joystick sensitivity, and save custom profiles — all synced automatically.

Instant Plug-and-Play

USB HID standard means zero driver installs. Works on every PC out of the box.

Hand-Based Layout

Ergonomic button placement modeled around natural hand positioning, not legacy gamepad conventions.

Built to Evolve

Designed for continuous iteration — future-ready with expandable compatibility and magnetic quick-swap upgrades on the horizon.
