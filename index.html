Why We Built This

Gaming is connection.
Everyone deserves access.

Ms. Qu used to play video games weekly with friends and family. But as her Parkinson's worsened, so did her ability to use a controller or keyboard—and with that, she lost a form of connection.

She's far from alone. Traditional controllers are designed for performance, not accessibility: clustered buttons, sensitive joysticks, and no flexibility. GameChanger was built to change that.

How It Works

Powered by a Pro Micro.
Controlled by you.

  • 01 Each button and joystick maps to a standard keyboard key via the Pro Micro microcontroller.
  • 02 USB HID protocol ensures instant recognition on any PC — no setup required.
  • 03 Open the companion app to remap inputs, tune sensitivity, or load a saved profile.
  • 04 Functions exactly like a traditional controller or keyboard — full game compatibility.
